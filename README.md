## ⚠️ Known Technical & Domain Constraints

- **Executive Voice Preservation:** Captures distinctive phrasing, professional vocabulary, and leadership perspective of the named executive.
- **Feed Algorithm Dwell Time:** Formats posts with strong 2-line hooks, progressive line breaks, and clear call-to-conversation questions.
- **Compliance with Corporate Disclosures:** Prevents sharing of non-public forward-looking corporate figures or material enterprise news.
